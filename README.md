## My Favorite Movie: *Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse*

**Release year:** 2018  
**Genre:** Animated superhero, action, and science fiction

### About the Movie

*Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse* follows Miles Morales, a teenager from Brooklyn who becomes Spider-Man. After a machine opens a path between different dimensions, Miles meets other versions of Spider-Man and works with them to protect their worlds.

### Why I Like It

- The animation has a colorful comic-book style.
- Miles grows into his role as a hero while facing challenges and doubts.
- The different Spider-people bring their own personalities and humor to the story.
- Its message about finding your own way to become a hero is inspiring.

### Main Characters

| Character | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Miles Morales | A Brooklyn teenager learning to become Spider-Man |
| Peter B. Parker | An older Spider-Man who mentors Miles |
| Gwen Stacy | A skilled Spider-Woman from another dimension |
| Kingpin | The main antagonist of the movie |

### My Rating

**Rating: 9/10**

> The movie reminds me that anyone can wear the mask and make a difference.

### Who Should Watch It?

1. People who enjoy superhero stories.
2. Fans of creative animation and comic books.
3. Viewers looking for a fun movie with an encouraging message.
